# harnessNovel

## Lead skill and workers

Do the work yourself. Do not invoke the `lead` skill, and do not brief CLI workers (`grok -p`, `codex exec`), unless the job is complex: it will span **5 or more files**.

A regex tweak, a single-function fix, a test file, a prompt edit, or a docs change is not a lead job. Implement it in this session.

Skip `/lead` as well for questions, explanations, lookups, and anything that needs tools a worker cannot use.
