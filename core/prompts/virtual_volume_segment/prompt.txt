【Volume-division basis】

Prefer story-unit boundaries. A plot unit (【ArcN: Chapters X-Y】) is itself a natural cut of the story arc, so volume boundaries should fall on plot-unit boundaries first.

On that basis, use the following signals to judge volume boundaries:
1. A large conflict is resolved: a core conflict that lasted across multiple segments reaches a staged close
2. A new stage begins: entering a new map, a new realm, or a new faction's range
3. An important character exits or enters: a core supporting character leaves, or a new core character appears
4. A time skip: story time clearly jumps
5. A qualitative change in the protagonist's identity or status: from outer-sect disciple to inner-sect, from rogue cultivator to sect master, and so on
6. A switch in conflict direction: external conflict turns to internal cultivation, or the reverse

【Volume-division requirements】

1. Each volume is no fewer than 60 chapters, usually 60-150 chapters. Completeness of the plot arc comes first; equal length is not required
2. Each volume should contain a relatively complete large arc (opening, development, climax, close), made of multiple plot units
3. Volume boundaries should fall on plot-unit boundaries first. Do not split one plot unit across two volumes
4. Give each volume a 3-8-word volume title that summarizes this volume's core event or image
