【Outline-structuring requirements】
Summarize the full-book outline in the following six blocks.

I. Positioning and core setting
1. Genre positioning: subject type, target-reader profile, payoff-density style.
2. Worldview skeleton: core worldview, faction landscape, power-system hierarchy.
3. Golden finger (the protagonist's core advantage): what it is, how it works, and how it evolves in the early, middle, and late book.

II. Core payoffs
The 3-5 most important climax scenes in the book. For each, mark:
1. The volume and chapter range where it occurs.
2. The earlier suppression and setup (crossbow theory).
3. The turning mechanism at the explosion.
4. The gains and growth after the payoff.

III. Character outline
1. Protagonist: name, identity and background, personality traits, core ability system, complete growth path (key nodes from weak to strong).
2. Inner circle: important supporting characters close to the protagonist, with identity, function, and relationship evolution marked.
3. Antagonists: primary antagonists and secondary antagonists, with core motive, the nature of the conflict with the protagonist, and the final outcome marked.

IV. Plot outline
1. Story synopsis (summarize the full-book mainline in 100 words or fewer).
2. Mainline major conflict: the ultimate threat that runs through the book, and why the protagonist cannot escape it.
3. Core plot arcs by volume. For each volume, mark:
   core conflict direction, protagonist goal, key upgrades, fame battle, peak battle, ending suspense.

V. Foreshadowing ledger
1. The 5-8 most important long-line foreshadowing items in the book: planting location, reveal location, function.
2. Core information-gap design: key information asymmetry between the reader's viewpoint and the characters' viewpoint.

VI. Growth system
The protagonist's complete path from weak to strong: key milestones of realm breakthroughs, cultivation-art evolution, resource acquisition, and status climb.

VII. Cultivation-system overview
A complete survey of the cultivation / power system:
1. Realm division: all realm names from low to high, and the power performance of each realm (what it can do, what it cannot do).
2. Breakthrough conditions: what each realm breakthrough requires (resources, comprehension, opportunity, and so on).
3. Cultivation arts and martial skills: names, grades, and effects of all cultivation arts and martial skills that appear in the book.
4. Combat-power reference: combat-power gaps between realms (for example the possibility and conditions of fighting above one's realm).
5. Special systems: other power sources besides the main cultivation system (demon cultivation, ghost cultivation, artifact cultivation, and so on).
6. Lifespan and realm: lifespan range of each realm.
