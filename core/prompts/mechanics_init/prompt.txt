Mechanics here is not a "system panel" that every novel must have. It is the rule layer in the novel that needs to be tracked or calculated stably.

Possible modes:
1. none: no mechanics layer. Suitable for novels driven mainly by plot, emotion, intellect, identity mismatch, or social games, and that do not depend on numerical settlement.
2. light_state: no visible system panel, but key state still needs internal tracking. Suitable for novels that must stay consistent on cultivation realm, resources, injuries, identity, relationships, favors owed, foreshadowing state, and similar.
3. explicit_mechanics: explicit system / game / panel / quests / XP / points / skill trees / lord resources, and similar. Numbers and state need to be maintained by the program; the prose must not let the model invent the math.
