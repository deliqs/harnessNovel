【Light-reskin requirements】

Your task is to make a light adjustment on top of the reference volume outline, not to design from zero.

Must keep:
1. This volume's core conflict direction and three-act rhythm
2. Payoff positions and the tension curve
3. Key turning points and foreshadowing nodes
4. Timing and function of character appearances

Required depth of adjustment (critical):

A light reskin is not find-and-replace. You must apply three layers at once:

Layer one: name replacement. Use names from 【New novel this-volume worldview】. Do not keep names from the reference novel.

Layer two: method replacement. When a plot's method of execution has a source-text fingerprint (some unique way of solving the problem), you must reach the same plot function by a different method. The new method should use the new novel's golden finger or worldview traits. For example: if the reference novel tames demon beasts with "military drill + ideology class", the new novel can change that to "bloodline contract + carrot and stick" or another method that fits the new worldview.

Layer three: causal-chain adjustment. Change the causal logic of how events happen. For example: an NPC professor becomes a golden-finger awakening; being pulled in passively becomes laying a scheme actively; solving it through connections becomes crushing it with power.

Judgment standard: after all names are deleted, if the two passages are still almost identical, the rewrite is not deep enough and must be reworked.

Forbidden operations:
1. Changing the core plot rhythm and turning points
2. Scrambling payoff positions and the tension curve
3. Rename-only find-and-replace (must be paired with layer-two and layer-three rewriting)

Boundary between new settings and reference notes (critical):
1. Body settings in 【New novel settings】, 【Foreshadowing tracker】, 【Character roster】, and 【Three-act structure】 must not retain reference-novel proper nouns, original-world historical events, original-world religious systems, or original-world causality.
2. Only in sentences of "matching reference novel" or "mapping note" may reference-novel proper nouns appear, and you must state clearly how they have already been replaced.
3. If a reference-novel plot has no natural counterpart in the new world, do not hard-invent an equivalent character. Write it as a function substitute, a merge, a deletion, or a delay, and state the reason.
4. The foreshadowing tracker must record the new novel's real foreshadowing. Do not freeze reference-novel foreshadowing as new-novel foreshadowing.
5. Self-check before output: if a reference term appears in a body-setting paragraph that is not "matching reference novel / mapping note", you must rewrite it.
